## Technical Details

# changestream · Small-Business Data Console

**changestream** is a browser-based, privacy-first data analysis and business intelligence console designed for small businesses, freelancers, and operators. Drop in spreadsheets (`.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.csv`) to instantly explore data, profile columns, visualize trends, monitor KPIs, and collaborate with a built-in AI analyst.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📁 1. Client-Side Spreadsheet Ingestion
- **Formats Supported**: `.xlsx`, `.xls`, and `.csv`.
- **Multi-Sheet Support**: Automatically detects and switches between multiple worksheets.
- **Privacy-First Processing**: All parsing happens locally in your browser using SheetJS. Raw spreadsheet rows never leave your machine — only compact summary profiles are sent to the AI analyst when requested.
